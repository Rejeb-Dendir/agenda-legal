Account and Data Deletion Request

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · App: Agenda by Ansawa Tech

If you want your Agenda account and associated company workspace data deleted, you can request deletion using either method below. We review each request before permanently removing data.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In the Agenda app (recommended)

  1. Open the Agenda app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. In the Data section, tap Delete account.
  4. On Request permanent deletion, choose a reason (optional details if you want), then tap Submit deletion request.
  5. Confirm when prompted to submit the request.

Your company stays active until the request is approved. While a request is pending, you can cancel it from the same screen. You will receive confirmation once the review is complete.

Option 2 — By email

Email ansawatech1@gmail.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request.

Include:

  • The email address used for your Agenda account;
  • Your company / workspace name (if applicable);
  • A clear statement that you want the account and associated data deleted.

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

What data is deleted

After we approve your request, we permanently delete or de-identify:

  • Your user profile and authentication credentials;
  • Company / workspace membership and role information;
  • Business workspace records stored for that account/company (such as products, inventory, sales orders, production records, cashbook entries, uploaded images, and related operational data), subject to the retention notes below.

What may be retained (and for how long)

  • Billing and payment-proof records (for example subscription status or receipt images) may be kept as required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance — typically up to 30 days after approval, or longer if the law requires.
  • Security / abuse logs needed to protect the service may be retained for a limited period.
  • Data stored only on your device (for example local preferences) is removed when you uninstall the app.

Processing time

We aim to review deletion requests promptly. Once approved, deletion or de-identification of eligible data is completed within a reasonable period (generally within 30 days of approval), unless a longer retention period applies as stated above.
