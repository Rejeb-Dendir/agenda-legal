1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Agenda (“Agenda,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our business management application and related services (the “Service”).

Agenda is operated by Ansawa Tech. For privacy questions, contact us at ansawatech1@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • The Agenda mobile applications (including Android and iOS);
  • Any related web or desktop clients we make available; and
  • Account registration, authentication, billing, and support related to the Service.

Agenda is designed for businesses (for example, production, inventory, sales, team, and financial workflows). When a company administrator invites team members, that company controls much of the business content stored in its workspace.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Name and email address;
  • Password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we do not store plaintext passwords);
  • Authentication data if you sign in with Google (such as your Google account identifier and basic profile details Google shares with us);
  • Company or workspace name and related organization settings;
  • Role, membership, and permission information within a company;
  • Contact details you choose to add (for example phone or website fields in company or member profiles).

3.2 Business content you and your team provide

Depending on how your company uses Agenda, the Service may store business operational data such as:

  • Locations, products, categories, stock, and inventory records;
  • Production / order workflow data and related history;
  • Sales orders, payments status, customers, and cashbook entries;
  • Team, attendance, tasks/todos, and activity or audit logs;
  • Reports and exported files you generate (for example PDF or spreadsheet exports);
  • Images and files you upload (for example product photos, category images, or billing payment receipts/screenshots).

Camera or photo-library access is used only when you choose to capture or select an image. We do not access your camera or gallery in the background.

3.3 Billing and subscription information

If your company uses paid plans, we may process:

  • Subscription plan, billing status, and access state;
  • Payment submission details you provide for manual review (including receipt images);
  • Records needed to approve, reject, or audit billing requests.

Unless stated otherwise in-product, card payments may be handled outside Agenda or through processes we communicate to you. We only collect what is needed to manage subscription access.

3.4 Device, app, and technical information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate the Service, such as:

  • App version and basic device/platform information;
  • Authentication session tokens and security-related logs;
  • Local preferences stored on your device (for example theme or language settings via on-device storage);
  • Diagnostic information related to connectivity or failed requests where needed to keep the Service reliable.

We do not currently use third-party advertising SDKs or sell personal information for advertising.

3.5 Information from third-party services

We use trusted processors to run the Service, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage, and related backend services;
  • Google — if you use Google Sign-In;
  • Platform providers such as Google Play / Apple for distribution and in-app update checks where applicable.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • Enable multi-user company workspaces, roles, and permissions;
  • Process business workflows you initiate (inventory, sales, production, reporting, etc.);
  • Handle billing, subscription access, and payment-proof review;
  • Respond to support requests and service notices;
  • Enforce our Terms of Use and prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where data-protection laws require a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you or your company requested;
  • Legitimate interests — such as securing the Service, preventing abuse, and improving reliability, where those interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • Consent — where required (for example certain optional permissions or communications);
  • Legal obligation — when we must retain or disclose information to comply with law.

6. How we share information

We may share information in these situations:

  • Within your company workspace — members with appropriate permissions can view business content according to roles configured by your company;
  • Service providers — processors such as Supabase and Google that help us host, authenticate, and operate the Service, under contractual and technical safeguards;
  • Legal and safety — if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or Agenda;
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Retention

We retain personal and business information for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain company workspaces, meet legal/accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

If you request account or company deletion, we review the request as described in the app. After approval, we delete or de-identify associated data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records (for example billing, fraud prevention, or legal compliance). Local data on your device can usually be removed by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Service, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, authentication protections, and infrastructure security provided by our hosting partners. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong unique password and protect devices used to access Agenda.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and role, you may have rights to:

  • Access or correct personal information;
  • Request deletion of your account or company data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Export certain information you provided.

Company administrators control much of the business data in a workspace. If you are a team member, some requests may need to go through your company admin first.

You can request account/company deletion from in-app Settings (subject to our review process). You may also email ansawatech1@gmail.com.

You can revoke Google Sign-In access from your Google account permissions at any time. You can deny camera/photo permissions in your device settings; some features that require images will then be unavailable.

10. Children

Agenda is a business tool intended for adult users and organizational use. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. International transfers

We and our processors may store and process information in countries other than where you are located. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email where appropriate. Continued use of the Service after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
